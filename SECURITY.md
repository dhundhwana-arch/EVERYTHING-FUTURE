# Security, Scientific Safety & Dual-Use Policy

**Project Owner:** DIVYESH BAMBHANIYA  
**Last Updated:** 2026-11-19

## Scientific Responsibility & Dual-Use Safeguards

EVERYTHING FUTURE strictly prohibits harmful operational instructions across all scientific and engineering domains, including:
- **Biological & Chemical Safety:** No actionable synthesis, enhancement, or weaponization protocols for pathogens, toxins, or hazardous chemical agents.
- **Nuclear & Radiological Safety:** Nuclear and fusion modules cover peaceful energy physics, safety principles, and public agency standards only—never weapons design or enrichment operational instructions.
- **Cybersecurity Ethics:** Cybersecurity modules focus exclusively on defensive engineering, cryptography, risk quantification (CVSS), and standards compliance (NIST/ISO)—never exploit payloads or unauthorized intrusion tools.

## Reporting Issues
If you discover a broken safety boundary, inaccurate scientific claim, license conflict, or bug in any Python utility or validation script, flag the affected module as `UNVERIFIED` or `OUTDATED` and report it to the Project Owner.
