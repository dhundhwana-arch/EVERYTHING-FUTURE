# Permission Policy

**Original Creator & Project Owner:** DIVYESH BAMBHANIYA  
**Last Updated:** 2026-11-19

## How to Request Written Permission

Organizations, researchers, educators, startups, or individuals wishing to use, adapt, redistribute, or commercially deploy protected original materials from **EVERYTHING FUTURE** must request written authorization prior to use.

### Required Information in a Permission Request
1. **Requester Identity:** Full name, affiliation/institution, and contact details.
2. **Scope of Use:** Specific module IDs, files, scripts, or templates requested.
3. **Intended Purpose:** Educational, non-commercial academic research, government/public interest, or commercial deployment.
4. **Distribution Channel:** Internal use only, public redistribution, or derivative publication.
5. **Duration & Geography:** Planned timeframe and regions of deployment.

## Authorization Categories

| Category | Written Permission Required | Separate Commercial License | Attribution Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Personal offline study / evaluation | See owner release terms | No | Yes |
| Academic / institutional redistribution | Yes | No | Yes |
| Open-source / derivative modification | Yes | No | Yes |
| Commercial / enterprise deployment | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Permission from DIVYESH BAMBHANIYA covers **only** original materials authored for EVERYTHING FUTURE. For linked third-party papers, datasets, standards (ISO/IEEE/BIS), or software libraries, request permission directly from the respective rights holder or follow their published license.
