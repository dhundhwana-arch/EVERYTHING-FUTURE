# Scientific Methodology & Epistemic Rigor Guide

**Source:** EVERYTHING FUTURE (v1.0.0) | **Owner:** DIVYESH BAMBHANIYA

## Core Principles of Research-Grade Inquiry

1. **Falsifiability:** Frame claims so concrete experimental or mathematical evidence can disprove them.
2. **Primary-Source Traceability:** Trace numbers and equations back to primary peer-reviewed papers, standards, or official agency releases—never secondary summaries alone.
3. **Distinguish Fact, Estimate, and Speculation:**
   - *Verified Fact:* Directly measured or published in primary peer-reviewed or official records.
   - *Engineering Estimate:* Model-derived projection with explicit assumptions and uncertainty bounds.
   - *Speculative Hypothesis:* Unproven future direction; must never be presented as established fact.
4. **Ablation & Control Discipline:** Change one variable at a time when attributing performance gains.
5. **Dual-Use Safety Responsibility:** Evaluate downstream misuse risks before publishing operational protocols in bio, chemical, radiological, or cybersecurity domains.
