# Advanced Materials — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `027_ADVANCED_MATERIALS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Scalable defect-free synthesis of 2D materials, room-temperature ambient-pressure superconductors, and recyclable thermosets.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Long lab-to-fab commercialization cycles (often 10–20 years) and synthesis reproducibility.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Autonomous self-driving materials labs, AI interatomic potentials, and solid-state battery ceramics.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Estimate composite elastic modulus bounds using Voigt (upper) and Reuss (lower) rule-of-mixtures.
