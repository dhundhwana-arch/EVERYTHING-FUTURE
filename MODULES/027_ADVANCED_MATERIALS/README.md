## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Structure-property relationships, DFT simulations, and composite engineering enable targeted material design.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** 300mm wafer-scale integration of 2D TMD transistors (ASML/TSMC/IMEC, 2026) and machine-learning crystal discovery.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Scalable defect-free synthesis of 2D materials, room-temperature ambient-pressure superconductors, and recyclable thermosets.
4. **Major Limitations:** Long lab-to-fab commercialization cycles (often 10–20 years) and synthesis reproducibility.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Autonomous self-driving materials labs, AI interatomic potentials, and solid-state battery ceramics.
6. **Resources Needed:** IMEC, CNRS, NIST materials data portals.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Estimate composite elastic modulus bounds using Voigt (upper) and Reuss (lower) rule-of-mixtures.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IMEC, CNRS, Max Planck Society, NIST
