# Advanced Materials — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `027_ADVANCED_MATERIALS` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **2D Material** | Crystalline solid consisting of a single or few atomic layers (e.g., graphene, MoS2, WS2). |
| **DFT (Density Functional Theory)** | Quantum-mechanical modeling method predicting electronic structure of materials. |
| **Metamaterial** | Engineered structure exhibiting electromagnetic or acoustic properties not found in nature. |
