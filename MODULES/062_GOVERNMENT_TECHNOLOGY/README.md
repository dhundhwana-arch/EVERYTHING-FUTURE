## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Open APIs, verifiable digital credentials, and transparent e-procurement reduce administrative friction and leakage.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Population-scale digital portals (e.g., India's DGFT trade portal) and open statistical data platforms.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Privacy-preserving citizen data governance, bridging rural accessibility gaps, and preventing vendor lock-in.
4. **Major Limitations:** Legacy mainframe debt, uneven cybersecurity capacity across local municipalities, and digital exclusion risks.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Open-source modular DPI stacks, zero-trust public cloud security, and multilingual voice-accessible civic services.
6. **Resources Needed:** DGFT India, NIST public-sector standards, and WTO digital trade guidelines.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Design a JSON schema and validation script for transparent open-government procurement records.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** DGFT India, NIST, WTO
