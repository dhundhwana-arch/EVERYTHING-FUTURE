**Module:** `062_GOVERNMENT_TECHNOLOGY` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DPI (Digital Public Infrastructure)** | Foundational open-standard digital systems for identity, payments, and verifiable data exchange. |
| **Open Data Portal** | Machine-readable public release of non-personal government statistics and budgets. |
| **Interoperability** | Ability of distinct public agencies to exchange verified records securely without manual re-entry. |
