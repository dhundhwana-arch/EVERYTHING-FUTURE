## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Commercial aviation achieves exceptional safety through redundancy, crew resource management, and strict maintenance standards.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Geared turbofans, satellite-based ADS-B tracking, and certified drop-in SAF blends.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Scalable zero-carbon long-haul propulsion and contrail-avoidance trajectory optimization.
4. **Major Limitations:** High cost of synthetic e-SAF and gravimetric energy limits of batteries for long-range jets.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Hydrogen-electric regional aircraft, open-fan ultra-high-bypass engines, and AI contrail routing.
6. **Resources Needed:** NASA Aeronautics research portals.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate true airspeed, density altitude, and takeoff ground-roll sensitivity to airport elevation.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NASA
