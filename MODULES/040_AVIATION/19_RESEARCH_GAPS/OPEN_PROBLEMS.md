# Aviation — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `040_AVIATION` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Scalable zero-carbon long-haul propulsion and contrail-avoidance trajectory optimization.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** High cost of synthetic e-SAF and gravimetric energy limits of batteries for long-range jets.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Hydrogen-electric regional aircraft, open-fan ultra-high-bypass engines, and AI contrail routing.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate true airspeed, density altitude, and takeoff ground-roll sensitivity to airport elevation.
