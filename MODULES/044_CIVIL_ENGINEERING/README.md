## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Finite-element structural analysis, geotechnical foundation design, and seismic building codes save lives worldwide.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Performance-based earthquake engineering, long-span cable-stayed bridges, and LC3 low-carbon cement blends.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Cost-parity zero-carbon cement/steel and automated structural health monitoring of aging bridges.
4. **Major Limitations:** Concrete accounts for ~7–8% of global CO2 emissions; retrofitting informal urban housing remains underfunded.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Carbon-mineralized concrete, fiber-optic bridge strain sensing, and modular prefabricated housing.
6. **Resources Needed:** USGS seismic hazard maps and national/ISO structural codes.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute simply supported beam maximum bending moment, shear force, and midspan deflection.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** USGS, NIST
