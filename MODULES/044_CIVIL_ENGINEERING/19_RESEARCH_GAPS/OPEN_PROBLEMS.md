# Civil Engineering — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `044_CIVIL_ENGINEERING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Cost-parity zero-carbon cement/steel and automated structural health monitoring of aging bridges.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Concrete accounts for ~7–8% of global CO2 emissions; retrofitting informal urban housing remains underfunded.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Carbon-mineralized concrete, fiber-optic bridge strain sensing, and modular prefabricated housing.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute simply supported beam maximum bending moment, shear force, and midspan deflection.
