## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Hydraulic network modeling, biological wastewater treatment, and energy-recovery RO desalination are mature.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Seawater RO energy consumption reduced near ~2.5–3.5 kWh/m^3 with pressure-exchanger recovery devices.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Zero-liquid-discharge brine valorization, PFAS destruction at scale, and affordable rural decentralized treatment.
4. **Major Limitations:** Membrane fouling, brine disposal ecology, and aging urban pipeline leakage.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Solar-coupled desalination, graphene/biomimetic membranes, and acoustic AI leak detection.
6. **Resources Needed:** FAO Land & Water resources and USGS water-science databases.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute osmotic pressure (van 't Hoff equation) and minimum thermodynamic desalination energy.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** FAO, USGS
