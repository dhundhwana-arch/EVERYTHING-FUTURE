# Water Engineering — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `037_WATER_ENGINEERING` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reverse Osmosis (RO)** | Pressure-driven membrane filtration separating dissolved salts from brackish or seawater. |
| **Specific Energy Consumption** | Electrical energy (kWh/m^3) required to produce desalinated or treated water. |
| **Non-Revenue Water** | Treated water lost to distribution leaks, metering inaccuracies, or unbilled consumption. |
