# Water Engineering — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `037_WATER_ENGINEERING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Zero-liquid-discharge brine valorization, PFAS destruction at scale, and affordable rural decentralized treatment.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Membrane fouling, brine disposal ecology, and aging urban pipeline leakage.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Solar-coupled desalination, graphene/biomimetic membranes, and acoustic AI leak detection.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute osmotic pressure (van 't Hoff equation) and minimum thermodynamic desalination energy.
