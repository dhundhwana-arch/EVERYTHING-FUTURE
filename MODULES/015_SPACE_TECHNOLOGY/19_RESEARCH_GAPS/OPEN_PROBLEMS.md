# Space Technology — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `015_SPACE_TECHNOLOGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Active space-debris remediation, radiation shielding for long-duration crewed missions, and in-situ resource utilization (ISRU).
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Launch cost per kg, orbital congestion in LEO, and radiation-induced electronics upsets.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** In-orbit servicing/refueling, optical deep-space laser links, and autonomous onboard navigation.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate circular orbital velocities, periods, and Hohmann transfer delta-v between LEO and GEO.
