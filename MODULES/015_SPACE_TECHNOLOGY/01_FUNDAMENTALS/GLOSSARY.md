# Space Technology — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `015_SPACE_TECHNOLOGY` | **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **LEO / GEO** | Low Earth Orbit (~160–2,000 km altitude) and Geostationary Orbit (~35,786 km altitude, 24h period). |
| **Hohmann Transfer** | Two-impulse elliptical transfer orbit minimizing delta-v between coplanar circular orbits. |
| **Delta-v** | Total velocity change required to execute an orbital maneuver, measured in m/s or km/s. |
| **Specific Impulse (Isp)** | Propulsion efficiency metric equal to thrust divided by propellant weight flow rate (seconds). |
