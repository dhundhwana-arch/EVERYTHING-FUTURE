## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Keplerian/patched-conic orbital mechanics, satellite communications, and robotic planetary exploration are highly mature.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Global navigation constellations, reusable launch stages, lunar south-pole missions, and multi-agency space coordination.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Active space-debris remediation, radiation shielding for long-duration crewed missions, and in-situ resource utilization (ISRU).
4. **Major Limitations:** Launch cost per kg, orbital congestion in LEO, and radiation-induced electronics upsets.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** In-orbit servicing/refueling, optical deep-space laser links, and autonomous onboard navigation.
6. **Resources Needed:** NASA/UNOOSA open mission registries, orbital mechanics calculators, and open Earth observation datasets.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate circular orbital velocities, periods, and Hohmann transfer delta-v between LEO and GEO.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NASA, ISRO, CNSA, UNOOSA
