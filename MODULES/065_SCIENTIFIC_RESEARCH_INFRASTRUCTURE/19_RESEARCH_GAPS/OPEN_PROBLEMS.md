# Scientific Research Infrastructure — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `065_SCIENTIFIC_RESEARCH_INFRASTRUCTURE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Sustainable long-term funding for open-source scientific software and equitable HPC access for developing nations.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Reproducibility crisis in several fields, paywalled journals, and rising cost of megascience facilities.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Federated open data clouds, registered reports, and automated computational reproducibility capsules.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Audit a research repository against FAIR data principles and generate a CITATION.cff metadata file.
