## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Large-scale international facilities (CERN, ITER) and open repositories (arXiv, Zenodo, OpenAlex) accelerate discovery.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** CERN's invention of the Web, Zenodo DOI minting, and Max Planck international partner-group networks.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Sustainable long-term funding for open-source scientific software and equitable HPC access for developing nations.
4. **Major Limitations:** Reproducibility crisis in several fields, paywalled journals, and rising cost of megascience facilities.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Federated open data clouds, registered reports, and automated computational reproducibility capsules.
6. **Resources Needed:** CERN, Zenodo, OpenAlex, Max Planck Society, CSIR India, RIKEN.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Audit a research repository against FAIR data principles and generate a CITATION.cff metadata file.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** CERN, Max Planck Society, CSIR India, RIKEN, CNRS
