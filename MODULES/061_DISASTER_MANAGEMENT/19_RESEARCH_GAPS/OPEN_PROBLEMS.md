# Disaster Management — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `061_DISASTER_MANAGEMENT` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Last-mile alerting in off-grid regions, compound hazard modeling (heat + grid failure), and resilient microgrids.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Alert fatigue, communication tower failure during storms, and underinvestment in pre-disaster mitigation.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Direct-to-cell satellite emergency alerts, AI flash-flood nowcasting, and drone rapid-assessment fleets.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Estimate extreme-event exceedance probability P = 1 - (1 - 1/T)^n over a structure's n-year design life.
