## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Seismic networks, weather radar, satellite flood mapping, and community evacuation drills drastically cut mortality.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Seconds-ahead earthquake early warning systems and rapid satellite damage mapping.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Last-mile alerting in off-grid regions, compound hazard modeling (heat + grid failure), and resilient microgrids.
4. **Major Limitations:** Alert fatigue, communication tower failure during storms, and underinvestment in pre-disaster mitigation.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Direct-to-cell satellite emergency alerts, AI flash-flood nowcasting, and drone rapid-assessment fleets.
6. **Resources Needed:** USGS natural hazards portals and NASA Earth-observation disaster feeds.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Estimate extreme-event exceedance probability P = 1 - (1 - 1/T)^n over a structure's n-year design life.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** USGS, NASA
