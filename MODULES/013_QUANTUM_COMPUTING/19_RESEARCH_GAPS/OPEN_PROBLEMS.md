# Quantum Computing — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `013_QUANTUM_COMPUTING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Scaling to thousands of fault-tolerant logical qubits for practical quantum chemistry and materials advantage.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Correlated hardware errors, cryogenic overhead, real-time decoder latency, and high physical-to-logical qubit ratios.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Quantum LDPC codes, neutral-atom reconfigurable arrays, trapped-ion photonic interconnects, and cryogenic CMOS.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Simulate single-qubit rotations and 2-qubit Bell-state entanglement in pure Python and verify measurement probabilities.
