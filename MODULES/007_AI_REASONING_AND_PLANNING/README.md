## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Classical search (A*, MCTS) and SAT/SMT solvers provide exact guarantees in structured domains; neural models excel at heuristic guidance.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Superhuman game-playing search systems and formal theorem-proving assistants integrated with proof verifiers.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Bridging informal natural-language problem statements with formal verifiable specifications.
4. **Major Limitations:** Exponential state-space blowup in classical planners; unfaithful intermediate rationales in neural models.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Hybrid neural-guided MCTS, formal proof synthesis (Lean/Coq ecosystems), and process-level verifiers.
6. **Resources Needed:** Formal logic datasets, planning domain definition benchmarks, and open proof verifiers.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compare BFS, A*, and greedy search path optimality and node expansions on 2D obstacle grids.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** Max Planck Society, arXiv CS.AI Community
