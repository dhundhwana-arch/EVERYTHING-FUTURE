## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Bridging informal natural-language problem statements with formal verifiable specifications.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Exponential state-space blowup in classical planners; unfaithful intermediate rationales in neural models.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Hybrid neural-guided MCTS, formal proof synthesis (Lean/Coq ecosystems), and process-level verifiers.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compare BFS, A*, and greedy search path optimality and node expansions on 2D obstacle grids.
