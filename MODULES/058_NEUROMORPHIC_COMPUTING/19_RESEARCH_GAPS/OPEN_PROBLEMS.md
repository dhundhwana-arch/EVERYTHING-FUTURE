# Neuromorphic Computing — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `058_NEUROMORPHIC_COMPUTING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Training SNNs to match deep Transformer accuracy on general tasks and standardizing neuromorphic compilers.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Device-to-device variability in analog memristors and immature software tooling compared to GPUs.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Hybrid digital-analog in-memory accelerators and event-driven edge sensor fusion.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Simulate event-driven spike encoding (rate vs latency coding) on synthetic time-series signals.
