## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Co-locating memory and compute dramatically reduces data-movement energy on sparse temporal signals.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Research neuromorphic chips and commercial event-based vision sensors for high-speed robotics.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Training SNNs to match deep Transformer accuracy on general tasks and standardizing neuromorphic compilers.
4. **Major Limitations:** Device-to-device variability in analog memristors and immature software tooling compared to GPUs.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Hybrid digital-analog in-memory accelerators and event-driven edge sensor fusion.
6. **Resources Needed:** IMEC nanoelectronics research and arXiv neuromorphic computing papers.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Simulate event-driven spike encoding (rate vs latency coding) on synthetic time-series signals.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IMEC, Max Planck Society
