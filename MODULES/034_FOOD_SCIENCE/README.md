## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Thermal pasteurization, HACCP safety controls, and macronutrient/micronutrient chemistry are globally standardized.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** High-pressure processing (HPP), fortified staple foods, and rapid pathogen PCR screening.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Eliminating ~30% global post-harvest food loss and scaling affordable sustainable protein alternatives.
4. **Major Limitations:** Energy intensity of refrigeration in hot climates and consumer acceptance of novel food matrices.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Solar-powered rural cold storage, precision fermentation proteins, and biodegradable active packaging.
6. **Resources Needed:** FAO food security databases and ICAR post-harvest engineering resources.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Model Arrhenius shelf-life acceleration across storage temperatures.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** FAO, ICAR India
