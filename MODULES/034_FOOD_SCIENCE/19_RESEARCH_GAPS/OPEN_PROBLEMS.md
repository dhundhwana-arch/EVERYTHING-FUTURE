# Food Science — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `034_FOOD_SCIENCE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Eliminating ~30% global post-harvest food loss and scaling affordable sustainable protein alternatives.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Energy intensity of refrigeration in hot climates and consumer acceptance of novel food matrices.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Solar-powered rural cold storage, precision fermentation proteins, and biodegradable active packaging.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Model Arrhenius shelf-life acceleration across storage temperatures.
