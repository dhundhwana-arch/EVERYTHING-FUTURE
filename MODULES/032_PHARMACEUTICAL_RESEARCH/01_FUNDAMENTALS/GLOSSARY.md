# Pharmaceutical Research — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `032_PHARMACEUTICAL_RESEARCH` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **ADMET** | Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion, and Toxicity profile of a drug candidate. |
| **Phase I–III Clinical Trials** | Staged human testing evaluating safety, dosing, and efficacy against standard of care. |
| **IC50** | Concentration of an inhibitor required to reduce biological target activity by 50%. |
