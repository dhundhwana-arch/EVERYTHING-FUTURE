# Pharmaceutical Research — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `032_PHARMACEUTICAL_RESEARCH` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Reducing >90% clinical attrition rate, combating antimicrobial resistance, and affordable rare-disease therapies.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Multi-billion-dollar R&D costs, poor animal-to-human translational fidelity, and complex supply chains.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Organs-on-a-chip toxicity screening, AI generative small-molecule screening, and adaptive clinical trial designs.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Model one-compartment oral drug concentration-time pharmacokinetics (Cmax, Tmax, half-life).
