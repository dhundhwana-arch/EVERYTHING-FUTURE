## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Randomized controlled trials, structure-based drug design, and GMP quality systems govern safe drug development.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Rapid mRNA vaccine platforms, monoclonal antibody therapeutics, and AlphaFold-assisted target modeling.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Reducing >90% clinical attrition rate, combating antimicrobial resistance, and affordable rare-disease therapies.
4. **Major Limitations:** Multi-billion-dollar R&D costs, poor animal-to-human translational fidelity, and complex supply chains.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Organs-on-a-chip toxicity screening, AI generative small-molecule screening, and adaptive clinical trial designs.
6. **Resources Needed:** PubMed/NCBI clinical registries and CSIR/academic medicinal chemistry publications.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Model one-compartment oral drug concentration-time pharmacokinetics (Cmax, Tmax, half-life).
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NCBI / NIH, CSIR India
