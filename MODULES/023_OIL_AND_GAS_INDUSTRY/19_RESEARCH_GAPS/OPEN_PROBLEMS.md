# Oil and Gas Industry — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `023_OIL_AND_GAS_INDUSTRY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Eliminating fugitive methane emissions and scaling economical subsurface CO2 sequestration.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Carbon intensity, stranded-asset transition risks, and environmental impact of extraction.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Geothermal repurposing of depleted wells, continuous optical methane monitoring, and blue-hydrogen CCS.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Estimate reservoir hydrocarbon volume using volumetric porosity and saturation equations.
