## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Subsurface seismic imaging, directional drilling, and global LNG logistics are mature industrial technologies.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Enhanced oil recovery, digital oilfield telemetry, and satellite-based methane leak detection.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Eliminating fugitive methane emissions and scaling economical subsurface CO2 sequestration.
4. **Major Limitations:** Carbon intensity, stranded-asset transition risks, and environmental impact of extraction.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Geothermal repurposing of depleted wells, continuous optical methane monitoring, and blue-hydrogen CCS.
6. **Resources Needed:** USGS Energy Resources Program assessments and public geological maps.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Estimate reservoir hydrocarbon volume using volumetric porosity and saturation equations.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** USGS Energy Resources Program
