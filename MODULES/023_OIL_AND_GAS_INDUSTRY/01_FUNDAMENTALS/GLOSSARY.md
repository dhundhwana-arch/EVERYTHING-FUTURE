| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reservoir Porosity** | Fraction of rock volume consisting of void spaces capable of holding fluids. |

| **CCS (Carbon Capture & Storage)** | Capturing industrial CO2 and injecting it into deep saline or depleted geological formations. |
