## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Open scholarly graphs (OpenAlex), preprint servers (arXiv), biomedical indexes (PubMed/NCBI), and Zenodo make research globally searchable.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Hundreds of millions of indexed works with open APIs and persistent DOIs.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Integrating negative results, multilingual non-English research, and standardized dataset provenance cards.
4. **Major Limitations:** Paper-mill spam, broken supplementary data links, and inconsistent dataset licensing.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Automated citation verification against Crossref/OpenAlex/PubMed and open dataset cards on Hugging Face/Zenodo.
6. **Resources Needed:** arXiv, PubMed/NCBI, OpenAlex, Zenodo, Hugging Face Datasets.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Query and deduplicate bibliographic CSV entries by DOI and verify metadata completeness offline.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** arXiv, NCBI/PubMed, CERN Zenodo, OpenAlex, Hugging Face
