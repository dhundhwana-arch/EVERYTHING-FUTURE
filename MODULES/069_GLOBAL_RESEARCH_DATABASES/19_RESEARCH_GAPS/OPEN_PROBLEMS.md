# Global Research Databases — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `069_GLOBAL_RESEARCH_DATABASES` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Integrating negative results, multilingual non-English research, and standardized dataset provenance cards.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Paper-mill spam, broken supplementary data links, and inconsistent dataset licensing.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Automated citation verification against Crossref/OpenAlex/PubMed and open dataset cards on Hugging Face/Zenodo.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Query and deduplicate bibliographic CSV entries by DOI and verify metadata completeness offline.
