## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Deterministic PLC/SCADA control, machine vision inspection, and robotic assembly lines are industrial standards.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** ROS 2 industrial drivers (FANUC), collaborative robots (ABB), and vibration-based predictive maintenance.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Zero-downtime OT cybersecurity, rapid low-batch reconfigurability, and legacy brownfield sensor retrofits.
4. **Major Limitations:** Proprietary protocol silos and high integration engineering costs for small/medium manufacturers.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** OPC UA interoperability, edge AI quality inspection, and digital-twin commissioning.
6. **Resources Needed:** ABB Robotics, FANUC, ROS 2, and NIST industrial control security guides.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute factory OEE and bottleneck throughput using Little's Law.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** ABB Robotics, FANUC, NIST
