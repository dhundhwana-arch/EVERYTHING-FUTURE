# Agricultural Technology — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `033_AGRICULTURAL_TECHNOLOGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Affordable sensor-driven irrigation for smallholder farms (<2 ha), nitrogen-fixing cereals, and heat-tolerant staple crops.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** High upfront hardware cost for smallholders, rural connectivity gaps, and groundwater depletion.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Low-cost IoT soil probes, solar agrivoltaics, CRISPR drought-tolerant traits, and AI pest diagnostics.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Simulate a 14-day FAO crop water-balance schedule triggering drip irrigation at allowable depletion.
