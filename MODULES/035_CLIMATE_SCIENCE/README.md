## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Greenhouse gas radiative physics, global temperature records, and ocean heat-content measurements are robustly verified.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Satellite Earth-observation time series (NASA) and coupled general circulation Earth system models.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Reducing uncertainty in cloud-aerosol feedbacks, ice-sheet tipping dynamics, and kilometer-scale regional rainfall.
4. **Major Limitations:** Computational expense of cloud-resolving global simulations and sparse polar/deep-ocean measurements.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Physics-informed AI weather/climate emulators and global methane/CO2 satellite constellations.
6. **Resources Needed:** NASA Earth science data, USGS hazards data, and Our World in Data climate series.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Solve a 0D planetary energy-balance radiative equilibrium model for varying albedo and emissivity.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NASA, USGS, IRENA
