# Climate Science — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `035_CLIMATE_SCIENCE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Reducing uncertainty in cloud-aerosol feedbacks, ice-sheet tipping dynamics, and kilometer-scale regional rainfall.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Computational expense of cloud-resolving global simulations and sparse polar/deep-ocean measurements.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Physics-informed AI weather/climate emulators and global methane/CO2 satellite constellations.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Solve a 0D planetary energy-balance radiative equilibrium model for varying albedo and emissivity.
