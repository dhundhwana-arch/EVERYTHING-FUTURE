# Climate Science — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `035_CLIMATE_SCIENCE` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Radiative Forcing** | Net change in Earth's energy balance (W/m^2) caused by greenhouse gases, aerosols, or albedo shifts. |
| **Equilibrium Climate Sensitivity** | Long-term global surface warming expected from a doubling of atmospheric CO2. |
| **Carbon Sink** | Natural or engineered reservoir (oceans, forests, soils) absorbing more carbon than it emits. |
