# Superintelligence Research — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `003_SUPERINTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Provable alignment guarantees under recursive self-improvement or complex multi-agent dynamics.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Absence of empirical testbeds for superhuman-level systems; reliance on proxy theoretical models.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Mechanistic interpretability, formal verification of neural policies, and international governance standards.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Simulate reward-hacking edge cases in deterministic gridworld reinforcement learning environments.
