## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Theoretical alignment research analyzes specification gaming, distributional shift, and oversight scaling; superintelligence itself remains unachieved and hypothetical.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Formalized safety taxonomies, red-teaming frameworks, and risk management standards such as NIST AI RMF.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Provable alignment guarantees under recursive self-improvement or complex multi-agent dynamics.
4. **Major Limitations:** Absence of empirical testbeds for superhuman-level systems; reliance on proxy theoretical models.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Mechanistic interpretability, formal verification of neural policies, and international governance standards.
6. **Resources Needed:** Interdisciplinary safety benchmarks, formal proof assistants, and open red-teaming protocols.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Simulate reward-hacking edge cases in deterministic gridworld reinforcement learning environments.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NIST, IEEE Standards Association, Academic AI Safety Labs
