# Emerging Technologies — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `070_EMERGING_TECHNOLOGIES` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Forecasting second-order societal impacts and building adaptive international safety standards without stifling beneficial science.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Hype inflation, premature deployment of unverified systems, and unequal global access to frontier tools.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Transparent TRL tracking, open safety benchmarks, and international scientific consortia.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Score an emerging technology from TRL 1 to TRL 9 using verifiable primary-literature evidence criteria.
