## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Rigorous technology assessment separates verified laboratory milestones (TRL 2–4) from commercial readiness (TRL 8–9).
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Cross-domain breakthroughs including 2D transistor integration, below-threshold quantum error correction, and AI structural biology.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Forecasting second-order societal impacts and building adaptive international safety standards without stifling beneficial science.
4. **Major Limitations:** Hype inflation, premature deployment of unverified systems, and unequal global access to frontier tools.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Transparent TRL tracking, open safety benchmarks, and international scientific consortia.
6. **Resources Needed:** WIPO, NIST, IEEE, IMEC, and EVERYTHING FUTURE's `RESEARCH_TRACKER.md`.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Score an emerging technology from TRL 1 to TRL 9 using verifiable primary-literature evidence criteria.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** WIPO, NIST, IEEE Standards Association, IMEC
