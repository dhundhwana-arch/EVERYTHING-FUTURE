# Mining and Mineral Processing — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `025_MINING_AND_MINERAL_PROCESSING` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ore Grade** | Concentration of valuable metal or mineral within mined rock (expressed in % or g/tonne). |
| **Comminution** | Crushing and grinding ore to liberate target mineral grains (major energy consumer in mining). |
| **Tailings** | Fine-grained residual slurry left after mineral extraction requiring engineered containment. |
