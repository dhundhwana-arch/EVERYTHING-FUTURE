# Mining and Mineral Processing — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `025_MINING_AND_MINERAL_PROCESSING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Low-energy ore comminution, dry-stack tailings safety, and urban mining/battery metal recycling.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Declining global ore grades, high water/energy consumption, and environmental/social governance challenges.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Direct lithium extraction (DLE), bioleaching with microbes, and sensor-based ore sorting.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate metallurgical mass recovery and grade-recovery curves from synthetic assay tables.
