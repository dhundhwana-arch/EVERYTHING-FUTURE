## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Mineral exploration geophysics, flotation chemistry, and USGS global commodity tracking are mature.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Autonomous haulage fleets and high-recovery hydrometallurgical extraction for copper, lithium, and rare earths.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Low-energy ore comminution, dry-stack tailings safety, and urban mining/battery metal recycling.
4. **Major Limitations:** Declining global ore grades, high water/energy consumption, and environmental/social governance challenges.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Direct lithium extraction (DLE), bioleaching with microbes, and sensor-based ore sorting.
6. **Resources Needed:** USGS Mineral Resources Program and national geological surveys.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate metallurgical mass recovery and grade-recovery curves from synthetic assay tables.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** USGS
