# Logistics — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `051_LOGISTICS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Zero-emission heavy-duty long-haul freight and rural last-mile logistics in low-infrastructure regions.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Port congestion cascades, empty backhaul miles, and driver/labor shortages.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Autonomous yard trucks, shared-capacity freight marketplaces, and electric rail-intermodal corridors.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Solve a small Capacitated Vehicle Routing instance using savings heuristics in Python.
