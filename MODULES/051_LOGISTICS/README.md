## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Intermodal containerization, heuristic VRP solvers, and automated sortation centers drive global freight efficiency.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Warehouse mobile robotics (AMR), dynamic route optimization, and real-time GPS/temperature telemetry.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Zero-emission heavy-duty long-haul freight and rural last-mile logistics in low-infrastructure regions.
4. **Major Limitations:** Port congestion cascades, empty backhaul miles, and driver/labor shortages.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Autonomous yard trucks, shared-capacity freight marketplaces, and electric rail-intermodal corridors.
6. **Resources Needed:** WTO logistics reports and open VRP benchmark instances.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Solve a small Capacitated Vehicle Routing instance using savings heuristics in Python.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** WTO, Boston Dynamics
