## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Nuclear fission physics, radiation dosimetry, and IAEA safety standards underpin global civil power and medical imaging.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** High-capacity-factor carbon-free electricity generation and precision radioisotope cancer diagnostics.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Cost-competitive modular reactor deployment, long-term geological repository siting, and closed fuel-cycle economics.
4. **Major Limitations:** High upfront capital costs, long construction timelines, and stringent regulatory licensing.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), accident-tolerant fuels, and accelerator-driven transmutation research.
6. **Resources Needed:** IAEA WISER/safety databases and CERN nuclear physics open data.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Model multi-isotope Bateman radioactive decay chains numerically (educational physics only).
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IAEA, CERN
