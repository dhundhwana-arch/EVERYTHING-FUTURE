# Nuclear Science — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `020_NUCLEAR_SCIENCE` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Binding Energy** | Energy required to disassemble an atomic nucleus into its constituent protons and neutrons. |
| **Passive Safety** | Reactor design relying on natural physical laws (gravity, convection) rather than active power for cooling. |
| **Half-Life** | Time required for half of the radioactive nuclei in a sample to undergo decay. |
