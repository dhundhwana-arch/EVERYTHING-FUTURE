# Nuclear Science — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `020_NUCLEAR_SCIENCE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Cost-competitive modular reactor deployment, long-term geological repository siting, and closed fuel-cycle economics.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** High upfront capital costs, long construction timelines, and stringent regulatory licensing.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), accident-tolerant fuels, and accelerator-driven transmutation research.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Model multi-isotope Bateman radioactive decay chains numerically (educational physics only).
