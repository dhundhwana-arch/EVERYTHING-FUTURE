# Satellite Technology — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `018_SATELLITE_TECHNOLOGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Autonomous collision avoidance, inter-satellite optical routing, and sustainable end-of-life deorbiting.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Radiation degradation of commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) electronics and radio-spectrum crowding.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Onboard AI edge processing of Earth-observation imagery and laser inter-satellite links.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute satellite ground-track revisit intervals and link-budget free-space path loss.
