## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** SmallSat/CubeSat standardization and phased-array Ka/Ku-band payloads have democratized space access.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Global broadband LEO constellations and daily multispectral/SAR Earth observation.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Autonomous collision avoidance, inter-satellite optical routing, and sustainable end-of-life deorbiting.
4. **Major Limitations:** Radiation degradation of commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) electronics and radio-spectrum crowding.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Onboard AI edge processing of Earth-observation imagery and laser inter-satellite links.
6. **Resources Needed:** UNOOSA satellite registry and NASA open Earth-data archives.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute satellite ground-track revisit intervals and link-budget free-space path loss.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NASA, ISRO, CNSA, UNOOSA
