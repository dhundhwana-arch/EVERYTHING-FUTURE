**Module:** `018_SATELLITE_TECHNOLOGY` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CubeSat (1U)** | Standardized 10x10x10 cm nanosatellite form factor enabling low-cost space access. |
| **ADCS** | Attitude Determination and Control System using star trackers, reaction wheels, and magnetorquers. |
| **Sun-Synchronous Orbit** | Polar orbit passing over any point on Earth at the same local solar time for consistent imaging. |
