# Environmental Engineering — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `060_ENVIRONMENTAL_ENGINEERING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Microplastic remediation, scalable direct air capture economics, and circular electronics recycling.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Measurement gaps in diffuse industrial emissions and high cost of end-of-pipe abatement.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Low-cost distributed air-quality sensor networks, enzymatic plastic degradation, and industrial symbiosis.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute a cradle-to-gate carbon footprint inventory for a multi-material product using emission factors.
