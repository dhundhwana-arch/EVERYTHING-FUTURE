## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Air scrubbing, sanitary landfill engineering, and ISO 14040 LCA methodology are established worldwide.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Dramatic reductions in urban sulfur/lead pollution and industrial ISO 50001 energy management (e.g., CERN).
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Microplastic remediation, scalable direct air capture economics, and circular electronics recycling.
4. **Major Limitations:** Measurement gaps in diffuse industrial emissions and high cost of end-of-pipe abatement.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Low-cost distributed air-quality sensor networks, enzymatic plastic degradation, and industrial symbiosis.
6. **Resources Needed:** USGS environmental science, CERN ISO 50001 reports, and FAO sustainability portals.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute a cradle-to-gate carbon footprint inventory for a multi-material product using emission factors.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** USGS, CERN, FAO, IRENA
