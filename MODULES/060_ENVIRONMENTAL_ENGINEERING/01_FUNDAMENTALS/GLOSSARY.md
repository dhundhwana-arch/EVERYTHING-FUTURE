# Environmental Engineering — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `060_ENVIRONMENTAL_ENGINEERING` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **LCA (Life-Cycle Assessment)** | Cradle-to-grave quantitative accounting of a product's energy, water, and emissions footprint. |
| **PM2.5** | Fine particulate matter <= 2.5 micrometers capable of penetrating deep into human lungs. |
| **Bioremediation** | Use of naturally occurring microorganisms to break down environmental pollutants. |
