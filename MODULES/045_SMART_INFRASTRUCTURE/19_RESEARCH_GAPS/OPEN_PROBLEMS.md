# Smart Infrastructure — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `045_SMART_INFRASTRUCTURE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Privacy-preserving civic sensing, interoperable open standards, and financing upgrades in developing cities.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Vendor lock-in, cybersecurity attack surfaces on municipal OT networks, and sensor maintenance overhead.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Open urban data standards, microgrid resilience hubs, and AI-assisted urban flood forecasting.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Model peak-shaving battery dispatch for a municipal microgrid load profile.
