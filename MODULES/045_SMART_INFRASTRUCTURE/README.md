## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Automated metering, adaptive traffic signal control, and GIS asset management are deployed across major cities.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Real-time transit feeds, smart LED streetlighting, and automated grid fault isolation.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Privacy-preserving civic sensing, interoperable open standards, and financing upgrades in developing cities.
4. **Major Limitations:** Vendor lock-in, cybersecurity attack surfaces on municipal OT networks, and sensor maintenance overhead.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Open urban data standards, microgrid resilience hubs, and AI-assisted urban flood forecasting.
6. **Resources Needed:** IEEE smart-city standards and NIST cyber-physical systems frameworks.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Model peak-shaving battery dispatch for a municipal microgrid load profile.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IEEE Standards Association, NIST
