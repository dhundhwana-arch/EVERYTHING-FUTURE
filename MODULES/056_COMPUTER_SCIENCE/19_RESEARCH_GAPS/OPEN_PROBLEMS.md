## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** P vs NP, scalable Byzantine fault tolerance at low energy, and provably bug-free large software systems.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Software complexity growth, memory-bandwidth wall, and legacy security debt.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Memory-safe systems programming (Rust), WebAssembly sandboxing, and verified compilers.
