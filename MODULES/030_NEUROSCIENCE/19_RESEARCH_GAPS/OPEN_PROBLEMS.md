# Neuroscience — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `030_NEUROSCIENCE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Mechanistic basis of consciousness, long-term memory consolidation, and curative therapies for neurodegeneration.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Spatial-temporal resolution tradeoffs in non-invasive human brain imaging and immense biological complexity.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Multimodal neuroimaging, organoid disease modeling, and biologically grounded computational neuroscience.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Simulate a Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) neuron firing rate vs input current in Python.
