## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Hodgkin-Huxley membrane electrophysiology, fMRI/EEG imaging, and invertebrate connectomes are well established.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Complete adult fruit-fly connectome mapping and high-density Neuropixels electrophysiology recordings.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Mechanistic basis of consciousness, long-term memory consolidation, and curative therapies for neurodegeneration.
4. **Major Limitations:** Spatial-temporal resolution tradeoffs in non-invasive human brain imaging and immense biological complexity.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Multimodal neuroimaging, organoid disease modeling, and biologically grounded computational neuroscience.
6. **Resources Needed:** PubMed/NCBI neuroscience archives and open connectomics datasets.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Simulate a Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) neuron firing rate vs input current in Python.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NCBI / NIH, Max Planck Society, RIKEN
