# Neuroscience — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `030_NEUROSCIENCE` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Action Potential** | Rapid depolarization and repolarization spike propagating along a neuron's axon. |
| **Connectome** | Comprehensive structural map of synaptic connections within a nervous system. |
| **Synaptic Plasticity** | Activity-dependent strengthening or weakening of synapses underlying learning and memory. |
