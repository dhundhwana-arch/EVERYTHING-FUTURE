## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Operations research (EOQ, reorder-point models, linear programming) and ERP visibility underpin modern supply chains.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Multi-sourcing strategies and real-time container/shipment telemetry.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Deep sub-tier (Tier 3–5) visibility, geopolitical shock resilience, and verified Scope 3 emissions accounting.
4. **Major Limitations:** Tradeoff between lean just-in-time cost efficiency and resilient buffer capacity.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Graph-based risk twins, regionalized friend-shoring hubs, and probabilistic multi-echelon inventory optimization.
6. **Resources Needed:** WTO World Trade Reports and DGFT trade analytics.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) and safety stock under stochastic lead-time demand.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** WTO, DGFT India
