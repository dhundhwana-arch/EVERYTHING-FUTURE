# International Supply Chains — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `050_INTERNATIONAL_SUPPLY_CHAINS` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bullwhip Effect** | Amplification of demand variability moving upstream from retail to raw-material suppliers. |
| **Safety Stock** | Buffer inventory held to protect against demand spikes or supplier lead-time uncertainty. |
| **Single-Point-of-Failure** | Concentrated geographic or vendor node whose disruption halts downstream global production. |
