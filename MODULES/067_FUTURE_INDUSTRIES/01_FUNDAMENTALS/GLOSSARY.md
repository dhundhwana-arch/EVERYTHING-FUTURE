# Future Industries — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `067_FUTURE_INDUSTRIES` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Learning Curve (Wright's Law)** | Empirical law observing unit cost falls by a constant percentage for each cumulative doubling of production. |
| **Techno-Economic Analysis (TEA)** | Integrated engineering and financial model evaluating whether an emerging technology can compete at scale. |
| **Industrial Convergence** | Fusion of AI, automation, advanced materials, and energy systems to create new markets. |
