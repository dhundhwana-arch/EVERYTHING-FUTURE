## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Techno-economic analysis and Wright's Law experience curves provide disciplined ways to evaluate nascent industries.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Rapid industrialization of utility-scale solar PV, EV batteries, commercial small-satellite constellations, and AI infrastructure.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Scaling green steel, commercial fusion power, cellular agriculture, and orbital manufacturing to mass-market unit economics.
4. **Major Limitations:** Speculative hype cycles, policy uncertainty, and multi-decade infrastructure turnover.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Modular factory-built energy/industrial units and AI-accelerated R&D compression.
6. **Resources Needed:** IRENA energy transition outlooks, WTO trade reports, and WIPO Green Technology Book.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Model Wright's Law unit-cost trajectory across cumulative production doublings with sensitivity bounds.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IRENA, WIPO, WTO
