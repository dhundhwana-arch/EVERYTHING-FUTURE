# Advanced Chip Packaging — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `012_ADVANCED_CHIP_PACKAGING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Sub-micron hybrid bonding yield, warpage control on large substrates, and kilowatt-class thermal dissipation.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Interposer reticle limits, testability of known-good dies (KGD), and complex thermo-mechanical reliability.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Glass core substrates, microfluidic die-level cooling, and standardized die-to-die interconnects.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute 1D thermal resistance stack (junction-to-ambient) for multi-die packages.
