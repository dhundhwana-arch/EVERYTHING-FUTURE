# Robotics and Humanoid Systems — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `008_ROBOTICS_AND_HUMANOID_SYSTEMS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** General-purpose human-level dexterous manipulation, energy-efficient bipedal autonomy, and safe unstructured human-robot interaction.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Actuator torque-to-weight limits, battery runtime, fragile tactile sensors, and high hardware cost.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Visuotactile foundation policies, quasi-direct-drive actuators, and standardized ROS 2 control interfaces.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Solve 2-link planar arm forward/inverse kinematics and verify workspace boundaries numerically.
