# Medical Technology — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `031_MEDICAL_TECHNOLOGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Low-cost diagnostics for rural clinics, non-invasive blood chemistry monitoring, and interoperable clinical records.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Regulatory approval timelines, clinical dataset bias, and hospital cybersecurity risks.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Multimodal wearable early-warning sensors, low-field portable MRI, and federated clinical AI.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute diagnostic ROC curves, positive predictive value (PPV), and Bayes' theorem post-test probabilities.
