## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** MRI/CT/ultrasound physics, minimally invasive surgical robotics, and regulated clinical validation pathways are mature.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** AI-assisted radiology triage, continuous glucose monitors, and portable handheld ultrasound devices.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Low-cost diagnostics for rural clinics, non-invasive blood chemistry monitoring, and interoperable clinical records.
4. **Major Limitations:** Regulatory approval timelines, clinical dataset bias, and hospital cybersecurity risks.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Multimodal wearable early-warning sensors, low-field portable MRI, and federated clinical AI.
6. **Resources Needed:** NCBI/PubMed clinical literature and IEEE/ISO medical device standards.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute diagnostic ROC curves, positive predictive value (PPV), and Bayes' theorem post-test probabilities.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NCBI / NIH, IEEE Standards Association
