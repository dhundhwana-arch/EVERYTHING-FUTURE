## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Continuous catalytic reactor design, distillation thermodynamics, and process safety (HAZOP) are well codified.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** High-efficiency integrated refinery-petrochemical complexes producing essential medical, industrial, and structural polymers.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Electrified zero-carbon steam crackers and economically viable closed-loop plastic depolymerization.
4. **Major Limitations:** High process heat emissions (~800°C+) and persistent plastic waste accumulation.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Electric catalytic crackers, bio-based monomers, and CO2-to-methanol electrocatalysis.
6. **Resources Needed:** CNRS Chimie and RIKEN catalytic chemistry publications.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Perform steady-state mass and energy balance calculations for a simplified catalytic reactor.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** CNRS Chimie, RIKEN CSRS, CSIR India
