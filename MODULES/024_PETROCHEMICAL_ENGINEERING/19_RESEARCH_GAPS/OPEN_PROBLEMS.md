# Petrochemical Engineering — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `024_PETROCHEMICAL_ENGINEERING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Electrified zero-carbon steam crackers and economically viable closed-loop plastic depolymerization.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** High process heat emissions (~800°C+) and persistent plastic waste accumulation.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Electric catalytic crackers, bio-based monomers, and CO2-to-methanol electrocatalysis.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Perform steady-state mass and energy balance calculations for a simplified catalytic reactor.
