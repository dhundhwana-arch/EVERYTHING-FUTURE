# Metallurgy — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `026_METALLURGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Zero-carbon primary steel/aluminum at parity cost and predicting fatigue life of 3D-printed metals.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** High energy intensity of blast furnaces and microstructural anisotropy in additive manufacturing.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** High-entropy alloys (HEAs), hydrogen-based DRI, and AI-guided alloy discovery.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute theoretical density and valence electron concentration (VEC) of multi-principal-element alloys.
