## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Thermodynamic CALPHAD modeling, steel/aluminum alloy design, and single-crystal turbine superalloys are mature.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Ultra-high-strength automotive steels, additive-manufactured titanium alloys, and pilot hydrogen-DRI steel plants.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Zero-carbon primary steel/aluminum at parity cost and predicting fatigue life of 3D-printed metals.
4. **Major Limitations:** High energy intensity of blast furnaces and microstructural anisotropy in additive manufacturing.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** High-entropy alloys (HEAs), hydrogen-based DRI, and AI-guided alloy discovery.
6. **Resources Needed:** NIST alloy databases and academic metallurgy literature.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute theoretical density and valence electron concentration (VEC) of multi-principal-element alloys.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NIST, CSIR India, Max Planck Society
