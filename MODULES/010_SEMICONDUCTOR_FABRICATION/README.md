## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** 300mm wafer fabrication integrates hundreds of ultra-clean photolithography, deposition, etch, and metrology steps.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** High-volume nanosheet/FinFET production (TSMC A16/A14 roadmaps) and 300mm 2D TMD research integration (IMEC/TSMC/ASML, 2026).
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Atomic-scale variability control, fab water/energy sustainability, and defect inspection at sub-nanometer scales.
4. **Major Limitations:** Extreme capital intensity ($10B+ per leading-edge fab) and geographically concentrated supply chains.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Selective atomic layer etching, robotic wafer maintenance (e.g., Lam Dextro), and PFAS-free fab chemistries.
6. **Resources Needed:** Process technology PDKs, cleanroom metrology datasets, and semiconductor engineering journals.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Model wafer die-per-wafer yield using Murphy/Poisson defect-density formulas.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** TSMC, Applied Materials, Lam Research, IMEC
