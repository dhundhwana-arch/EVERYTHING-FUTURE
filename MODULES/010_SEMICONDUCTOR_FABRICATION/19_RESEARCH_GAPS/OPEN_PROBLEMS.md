# Semiconductor Fabrication — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `010_SEMICONDUCTOR_FABRICATION` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Atomic-scale variability control, fab water/energy sustainability, and defect inspection at sub-nanometer scales.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Extreme capital intensity ($10B+ per leading-edge fab) and geographically concentrated supply chains.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Selective atomic layer etching, robotic wafer maintenance (e.g., Lam Dextro), and PFAS-free fab chemistries.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Model wafer die-per-wafer yield using Murphy/Poisson defect-density formulas.
