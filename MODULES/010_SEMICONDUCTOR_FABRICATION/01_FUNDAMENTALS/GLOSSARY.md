# Semiconductor Fabrication — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `010_SEMICONDUCTOR_FABRICATION` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wafer** | Ultra-pure monocrystalline silicon disc (typically 300mm diameter) serving as IC substrate. |
| **ALD (Atomic Layer Deposition)** | Self-limiting thin-film deposition achieving atomic-scale thickness control. |
| **Etch** | Selective chemical (wet) or plasma (dry) removal of wafer material to form patterns. |
| **Yield** | Percentage of functional dies produced per processed silicon wafer. |
