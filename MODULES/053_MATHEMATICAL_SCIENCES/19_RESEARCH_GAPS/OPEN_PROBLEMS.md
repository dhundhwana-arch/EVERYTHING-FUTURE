# Mathematical Sciences — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `053_MATHEMATICAL_SCIENCES` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Millennium Prize problems (P vs NP, Navier-Stokes smoothness, Riemann Hypothesis) and automated formal proof discovery.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Floating-point stability in ill-conditioned high-dimensional systems and curse of dimensionality.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** AI-assisted formal theorem proving, randomized numerical linear algebra, and topological data analysis.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Run 4th-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) integration on harmonic oscillator and decay ODEs and verify O(h^4) error convergence.
