# Global Trade and Export — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `049_GLOBAL_TRADE_AND_EXPORT` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Global interoperability of electronic Bills of Lading (eBL), carbon-border accounting, and SME export financing.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Non-tariff barriers, shifting export controls on dual-use tech, and complex multi-country rules of origin.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Paperless MLETR electronic trade documents, automated HS classification, and transparent supply-chain traceability.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute CIF/FOB landed import cost including freight, marine insurance, customs duty, and IGST/VAT.
