# Global Trade and Export — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `049_GLOBAL_TRADE_AND_EXPORT` | **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HS Code (Harmonized System)** | Internationally standardized 6-digit (8-digit ITC-HS in India) numerical product classification for customs tariffs. |
| **Incoterms 2020** | ICC standard trade terms (EXW, FOB, CIF, DDP) defining buyer/seller cost, risk, and insurance transfer points. |
| **Bill of Lading (B/L)** | Legal transport document issued by a carrier serving as receipt of goods, contract of carriage, and title. |
| **Letter of Credit (L/C)** | Bank guarantee assuring exporter payment upon presentation of compliant shipping documents. |
| **Certificate of Origin** | Official declaration certifying the country where exported goods were manufactured or substantially transformed. |
