## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Multilateral WTO tariff frameworks, HS classification, and Incoterms 2020 govern international merchandise trade.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Digitized customs portals (e.g., India's DGFT IEC/ITC-HS portal) and WTO Trade Facilitation Agreement implementation.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Global interoperability of electronic Bills of Lading (eBL), carbon-border accounting, and SME export financing.
4. **Major Limitations:** Non-tariff barriers, shifting export controls on dual-use tech, and complex multi-country rules of origin.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Paperless MLETR electronic trade documents, automated HS classification, and transparent supply-chain traceability.
6. **Resources Needed:** WTO trade portals, DGFT India export-import guides, and standalone Export Documentation Checklist & Landed-Cost Calculator.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute CIF/FOB landed import cost including freight, marine insurance, customs duty, and IGST/VAT.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** WTO, DGFT India
