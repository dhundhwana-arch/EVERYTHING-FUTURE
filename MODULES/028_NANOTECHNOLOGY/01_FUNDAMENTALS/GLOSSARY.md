# Nanotechnology — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `028_NANOTECHNOLOGY` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Quantum Confinement** | Discretization of electronic energy levels when particle size approaches the de Broglie wavelength. |
| **Nanoparticle** | Particle between 1 and 100 nm exhibiting size-dependent optical, catalytic, or biological properties. |
| **Self-Assembly** | Spontaneous organization of molecules or nanostructures into ordered patterns. |
