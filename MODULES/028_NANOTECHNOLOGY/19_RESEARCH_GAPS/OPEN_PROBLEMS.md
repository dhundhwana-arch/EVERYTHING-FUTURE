# Nanotechnology — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `028_NANOTECHNOLOGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Targeted nanomedicine crossing the blood-brain barrier and atomic-precision bottom-up manufacturing.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Nanotoxicology characterization, batch-to-batch uniformity, and high-throughput nanometrology.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** DNA origami nanocarriers, nanocatalysts for green hydrogen, and single-atom catalysis.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute surface-area-to-volume ratio scaling as spherical particle radius shrinks from 1 mm to 5 nm.
