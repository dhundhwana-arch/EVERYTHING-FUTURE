## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Lipid nanoparticles (LNPs), quantum dots, and nanoscale semiconductor patterning are commercially deployed.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** mRNA lipid nanoparticle delivery systems, quantum-dot displays, and sub-5nm transistor channels.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Targeted nanomedicine crossing the blood-brain barrier and atomic-precision bottom-up manufacturing.
4. **Major Limitations:** Nanotoxicology characterization, batch-to-batch uniformity, and high-throughput nanometrology.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** DNA origami nanocarriers, nanocatalysts for green hydrogen, and single-atom catalysis.
6. **Resources Needed:** NIST nanometrology standards and IMEC nanoelectronics publications.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute surface-area-to-volume ratio scaling as spherical particle radius shrinks from 1 mm to 5 nm.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IMEC, NIST, Max Planck Society
