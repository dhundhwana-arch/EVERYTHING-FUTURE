# Advanced Manufacturing — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `043_ADVANCED_MANUFACTURING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Real-time defect correction during metal 3D printing and high-throughput low-cost powder recycling.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Residual thermal stresses, post-processing costs, and anisotropic mechanical properties in AM parts.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Multi-laser LPBF, hybrid additive-subtractive cells, and AI-generated topology-optimized parts.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate CNC material removal rate (MRR) and machining cycle time from feed, speed, and depth of cut.
