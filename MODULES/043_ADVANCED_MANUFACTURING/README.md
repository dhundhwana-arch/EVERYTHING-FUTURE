## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** 5-axis CNC machining, injection molding, and metrology-grade additive manufacturing are commercially established.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Flight-certified 3D-printed rocket/turbine nozzles and closed-loop laser melt-pool monitoring.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Real-time defect correction during metal 3D printing and high-throughput low-cost powder recycling.
4. **Major Limitations:** Residual thermal stresses, post-processing costs, and anisotropic mechanical properties in AM parts.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Multi-laser LPBF, hybrid additive-subtractive cells, and AI-generated topology-optimized parts.
6. **Resources Needed:** NIST smart manufacturing and additive manufacturing standards.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate CNC material removal rate (MRR) and machining cycle time from feed, speed, and depth of cut.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NIST, ABB Robotics, FANUC
