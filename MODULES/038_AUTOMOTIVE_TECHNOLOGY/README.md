## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Chassis dynamics, functional safety (ISO 26262), and embedded real-time vehicle control are well established.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Software-defined zonal vehicle architectures and widespread Level 2/2+ highway driver assistance.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Safe all-weather Level 4/5 urban autonomy and formal validation of edge-case perception failures.
4. **Major Limitations:** Long-tail corner cases in unstructured traffic, sensor occlusion, and automotive cybersecurity.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** End-to-end verifiable driving policies, 4D imaging radar, and V2X cooperative safety messaging.
6. **Resources Needed:** IEEE automotive standards and ROS 2 autonomous vehicle stacks.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate stopping sight distance across vehicle speeds, reaction times, and road friction coefficients.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IEEE Standards Association, ROS 2 Community
