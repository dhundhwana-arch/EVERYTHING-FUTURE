## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Clearly stating what science does NOT yet know is essential to directing research effort productively.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Systematic cataloging of open problems across physics (quantum gravity, dark matter), math (P vs NP), and biology (aging, consciousness).
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Dozens of fundamental bottlenecks documented in `RESEARCH_INTELLIGENCE/RESEARCH_GAPS_DATABASE.csv`.
4. **Major Limitations:** Tendency of funding systems to favor incremental publishable work over high-risk foundational questions.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Focused research organizations (FROs), open prize challenges, and interdisciplinary AI-for-science collaborations.
6. **Resources Needed:** CERN, NASA, Max Planck Society, and `RESEARCH_INTELLIGENCE/RESEARCH_GAPS_DATABASE.csv`.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Draft a falsifiable research proposal targeting one sub-problem using `RESEARCH_INTELLIGENCE/TEMPLATES/RESEARCH_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md`.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** CERN, NASA, Max Planck Society, NIST
