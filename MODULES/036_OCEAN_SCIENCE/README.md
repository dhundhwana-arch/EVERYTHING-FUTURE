## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Satellite altimetry, autonomous profiling floats, and carbonate chemistry are foundational to modern oceanography.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Continuous upper-ocean temperature/salinity monitoring and autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) surveys.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** High-resolution deep-seafloor mapping, deep-ocean biodiversity inventories, and overturning circulation early warning.
4. **Major Limitations:** Extreme hydrostatic pressure, acoustic communication bandwidth limits, and high ship-time costs.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Swarms of solar/wave-powered autonomous surface/underwater gliders and environmental DNA (eDNA) sensing.
6. **Resources Needed:** NASA oceanography portals and USGS coastal/marine datasets.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate seawater hydrostatic pressure and density shifts across depth and salinity profiles.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NASA, USGS
