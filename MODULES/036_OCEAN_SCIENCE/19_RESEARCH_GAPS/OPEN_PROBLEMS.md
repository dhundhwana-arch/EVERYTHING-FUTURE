# Ocean Science — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `036_OCEAN_SCIENCE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** High-resolution deep-seafloor mapping, deep-ocean biodiversity inventories, and overturning circulation early warning.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Extreme hydrostatic pressure, acoustic communication bandwidth limits, and high ship-time costs.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Swarms of solar/wave-powered autonomous surface/underwater gliders and environmental DNA (eDNA) sensing.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate seawater hydrostatic pressure and density shifts across depth and salinity profiles.
