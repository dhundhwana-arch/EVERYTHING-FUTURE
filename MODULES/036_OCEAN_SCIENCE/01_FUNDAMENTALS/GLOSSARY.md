# Ocean Science — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `036_OCEAN_SCIENCE` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Thermohaline Circulation** | Global density-driven ocean current system governed by temperature and salinity gradients. |
| **Ocean Acidification** | Decrease in seawater pH driven by absorption of atmospheric CO2 forming carbonic acid. |
| **Bathymetry** | Measurement and mapping of seafloor depth and underwater topography. |
