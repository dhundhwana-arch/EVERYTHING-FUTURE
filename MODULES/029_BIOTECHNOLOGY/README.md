## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Next-generation sequencing, recombinant protein manufacturing, CRISPR gene editing, and AI protein structure prediction are established.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** AlphaFold 2 CASP14 breakthrough (Nature 2021), CRISPR-Cas9 clinical therapies, and open genomic repositories (NCBI).
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Predicting multi-protein dynamic complexes, safe in-vivo gene delivery to all tissues, and de-novo enzyme kinetics.
4. **Major Limitations:** Off-target editing risks, immunogenicity of delivery vectors, and strict biosafety/bioethics requirements.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Prime/base editing, AI-guided benign enzyme engineering for agriculture/recycling, and automated biofoundries.
6. **Resources Needed:** NCBI/PubMed open literature, AlphaFold DB, and safe bioinformatics sequence tools.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Run computational GC-content, reverse-complement, and codon translation on benign synthetic DNA strings.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NCBI / NIH, RIKEN CSRS, Max Planck Society, CSIR India
