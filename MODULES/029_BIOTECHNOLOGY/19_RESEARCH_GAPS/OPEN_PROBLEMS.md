# Biotechnology — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `029_BIOTECHNOLOGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Predicting multi-protein dynamic complexes, safe in-vivo gene delivery to all tissues, and de-novo enzyme kinetics.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Off-target editing risks, immunogenicity of delivery vectors, and strict biosafety/bioethics requirements.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Prime/base editing, AI-guided benign enzyme engineering for agriculture/recycling, and automated biofoundries.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Run computational GC-content, reverse-complement, and codon translation on benign synthetic DNA strings.
