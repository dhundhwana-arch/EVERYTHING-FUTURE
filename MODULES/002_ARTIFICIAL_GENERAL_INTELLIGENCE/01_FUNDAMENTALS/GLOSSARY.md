# Artificial General Intelligence — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `002_ARTIFICIAL_GENERAL_INTELLIGENCE` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Generalization** | Ability to apply learned principles to novel tasks outside the training distribution. |
| **World Model** | Internal predictive model of physical, causal, or social environment dynamics. |
| **Transfer Learning** | Reusing knowledge gained in one domain to accelerate learning in another. |
| **Compositional Reasoning** | Combining elementary concepts into novel multi-step solutions. |
