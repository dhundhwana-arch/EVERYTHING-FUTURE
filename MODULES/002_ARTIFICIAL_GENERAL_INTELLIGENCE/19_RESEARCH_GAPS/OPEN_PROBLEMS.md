# Artificial General Intelligence — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `002_ARTIFICIAL_GENERAL_INTELLIGENCE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Autonomous hypothesis generation, continual online learning without catastrophic forgetting, and grounded physical common sense.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Lack of consensus formal definition of AGI; benchmark saturation and contamination.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Active inference, hybrid symbolic-neural planners, and embodied simulation environments.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Benchmark multi-step algorithmic generalization on procedurally generated logic puzzles.
