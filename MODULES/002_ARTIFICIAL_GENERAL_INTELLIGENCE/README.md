## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Current frontier models display broad cross-domain competence but rely heavily on massive pretraining corpora rather than true causal world models.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Unified multimodal models handling text, code, images, and audio within shared architectures.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Autonomous hypothesis generation, continual online learning without catastrophic forgetting, and grounded physical common sense.
4. **Major Limitations:** Lack of consensus formal definition of AGI; benchmark saturation and contamination.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Active inference, hybrid symbolic-neural planners, and embodied simulation environments.
6. **Resources Needed:** Contamination-resistant evaluation suites and interdisciplinary cognitive-science benchmarks.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Benchmark multi-step algorithmic generalization on procedurally generated logic puzzles.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** Max Planck Society, NIST, arXiv AI Research Community
