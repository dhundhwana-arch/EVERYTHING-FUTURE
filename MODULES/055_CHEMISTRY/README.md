## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Thermodynamics, reaction kinetics, spectroscopy, and synthetic organic methodology are thoroughly established.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Asymmetric organocatalysis, cryo-EM molecular imaging, and automated high-throughput reaction screening.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Predicting reaction yields/selectivity in complex solvents and replacing precious-metal catalysts with earth-abundant elements.
4. **Major Limitations:** Environmental persistence of legacy fluorinated compounds (PFAS) and energy-intensive separations.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Photocatalysis, electro-organic synthesis, and AI-guided retrosynthetic planning.
6. **Resources Needed:** CNRS Chimie, RIKEN CSRS, and NIST chemistry webbooks.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute chemical equilibrium constants and Gibbs free energy (Delta G = Delta H - T*Delta S).
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** CNRS Chimie, RIKEN CSRS, NIST, CSIR India
