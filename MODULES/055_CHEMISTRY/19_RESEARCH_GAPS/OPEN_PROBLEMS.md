# Chemistry — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `055_CHEMISTRY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Predicting reaction yields/selectivity in complex solvents and replacing precious-metal catalysts with earth-abundant elements.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Environmental persistence of legacy fluorinated compounds (PFAS) and energy-intensive separations.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Photocatalysis, electro-organic synthesis, and AI-guided retrosynthetic planning.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute chemical equilibrium constants and Gibbs free energy (Delta G = Delta H - T*Delta S).
