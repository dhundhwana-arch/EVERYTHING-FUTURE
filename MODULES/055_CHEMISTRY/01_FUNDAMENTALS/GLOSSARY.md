# Chemistry — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `055_CHEMISTRY` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Activation Energy** | Minimum energy barrier reactants must overcome to form products (lowered by catalysts). |
| **Stoichiometry** | Quantitative relationship between masses and moles of reactants and products in balanced reactions. |
| **Green Chemistry** | Design of chemical products and processes minimizing hazardous substances and waste. |
