# Telecommunications — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `046_TELECOMMUNICATIONS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Terahertz/sub-THz propagation for 6G, ultra-low-energy base stations, and universal rural broadband.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Spectrum scarcity, high rural backhaul capex, and power consumption of dense millimeter-wave radios.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Open RAN (O-RAN), integrated sensing and communications (ISAC), and non-terrestrial network (NTN) convergence.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute Shannon channel capacity and Friis free-space path loss across frequency bands.
