## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Shannon information theory, LDPC/polar error-correcting codes, optical fiber transport, and 5G NR are mature.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Multi-terabit submarine optical cables, global 5G rollouts, and direct-to-device LEO satellite links.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Terahertz/sub-THz propagation for 6G, ultra-low-energy base stations, and universal rural broadband.
4. **Major Limitations:** Spectrum scarcity, high rural backhaul capex, and power consumption of dense millimeter-wave radios.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Open RAN (O-RAN), integrated sensing and communications (ISAC), and non-terrestrial network (NTN) convergence.
6. **Resources Needed:** IEEE 802/wireless standards and NIST communications technology lab.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Compute Shannon channel capacity and Friis free-space path loss across frequency bands.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IEEE Standards Association, NIST
