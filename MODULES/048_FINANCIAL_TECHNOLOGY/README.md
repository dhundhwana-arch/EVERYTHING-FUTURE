## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Instant mobile payment networks (such as UPI in India), ISO 20022 messaging, and cloud core banking have scaled globally.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Billions of low-cost instant digital transactions expanding financial inclusion across emerging and developed economies.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Low-cost instant cross-border settlement, real-time synthetic-identity fraud prevention, and privacy-preserving audits.
4. **Major Limitations:** Regulatory fragmentation across jurisdictions, cyber-fraud evolution, and systemic cloud concentration risk.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Interlinked fast-payment corridors, zero-knowledge compliance proofs, and explainable credit-risk models.
6. **Resources Needed:** WTO digital trade resources and NIST financial cybersecurity guidelines.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate loan amortization schedules, IRR, and Value-at-Risk (VaR) on synthetic return series.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** WTO, NIST
