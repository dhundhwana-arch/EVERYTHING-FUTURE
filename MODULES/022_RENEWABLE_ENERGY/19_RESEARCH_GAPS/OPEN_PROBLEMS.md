# Renewable Energy — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `022_RENEWABLE_ENERGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Affordable multi-day/seasonal storage, high-voltage transmission buildout, and 100% inverter-based grid stability.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Land-use siting, curtailment during peak solar hours, and supply-chain bottlenecks in transformers and critical minerals.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Perovskite-silicon tandem PV cells, sodium-ion/iron-air storage, enhanced geothermal systems, and smart demand response.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Model solar PV LCOE sensitivity to capital cost, discount rate, capacity factor, and annual panel degradation.
