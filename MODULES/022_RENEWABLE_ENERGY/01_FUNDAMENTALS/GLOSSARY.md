# Renewable Energy — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `022_RENEWABLE_ENERGY` | **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **LCOE** | Levelized Cost of Electricity: lifetime discounted system cost divided by lifetime discounted energy output ($/kWh). |
| **Capacity Factor** | Ratio of actual electrical output over a period to maximum possible output at rated nameplate power. |
| **Intermittency** | Temporal variability of solar/wind output requiring storage, transmission, or dispatchable backup. |
| **Grid-Forming Inverter** | Power electronics inverter capable of establishing voltage and frequency references without synchronous generators. |
