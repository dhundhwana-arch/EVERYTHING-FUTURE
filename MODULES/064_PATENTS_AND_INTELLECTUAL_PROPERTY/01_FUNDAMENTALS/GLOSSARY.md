# Patents and Intellectual Property — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `064_PATENTS_AND_INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Prior Art** | Any public disclosure prior to a patent filing date that may anticipate or render an invention obvious. |
| **PCT (Patent Cooperation Treaty)** | WIPO-administered treaty enabling unified international patent application across 150+ countries. |
| **Freedom to Operate (FTO)** | Legal/technical analysis assessing whether commercializing a product infringes active third-party patents. |
