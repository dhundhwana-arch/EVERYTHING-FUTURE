## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Legal frameworks for AI-assisted inventions, training-data copyright boundaries, and balanced essential-patent licensing.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** High cross-border patent prosecution costs for independent inventors and startups.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** AI-assisted prior-art search tools, defensive patent pools, and transparent open-hardware/dual-licensing models.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Run a structured prior-art keyword & CPC classification search checklist before drafting an invention disclosure.
