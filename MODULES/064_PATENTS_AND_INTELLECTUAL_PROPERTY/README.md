## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Novelty, non-obviousness (inventive step), and industrial applicability govern patent grants under WIPO/WTO TRIPS.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Global WIPO IP indicator tracking, PCT digital filing, and CSIR/university technology-transfer frameworks.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Legal frameworks for AI-assisted inventions, training-data copyright boundaries, and balanced essential-patent licensing.
4. **Major Limitations:** High cross-border patent prosecution costs for independent inventors and startups.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** AI-assisted prior-art search tools, defensive patent pools, and transparent open-hardware/dual-licensing models.
6. **Resources Needed:** WIPO treaties/indicators, WTO TRIPS portal, and CSIR IP documentation.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Run a structured prior-art keyword & CPC classification search checklist before drafting an invention disclosure.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** WIPO, WTO, CSIR India
