# Aerospace Engineering — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `016_AEROSPACE_ENGINEERING` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reynolds Number** | Dimensionless ratio of inertial to viscous forces governing laminar vs turbulent flow. |
| **Mach Number** | Ratio of vehicle speed to the local speed of sound. |
| **Carbon-Fiber Composite** | High strength-to-weight structural material widely used in modern airframes. |
