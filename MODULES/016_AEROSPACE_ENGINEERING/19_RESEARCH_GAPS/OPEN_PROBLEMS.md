# Aerospace Engineering — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `016_AEROSPACE_ENGINEERING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Low-emission commercial propulsion, hypersonic thermal protection materials, and urban air-traffic autonomy.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Battery energy density limits for long-haul electric flight and high certification costs.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Sustainable aviation fuels (SAF), hybrid-electric regional propulsion, and active laminar flow control.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate lift/drag polar curves and range via the Breguet range equation.
