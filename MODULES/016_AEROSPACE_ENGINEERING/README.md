## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Subsonic/supersonic aerodynamics, CFD modeling, and fly-by-wire avionics are deeply established.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** High-bypass turbofan efficiency gains, composite airliners, and autonomous aerial vehicles.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Low-emission commercial propulsion, hypersonic thermal protection materials, and urban air-traffic autonomy.
4. **Major Limitations:** Battery energy density limits for long-haul electric flight and high certification costs.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Sustainable aviation fuels (SAF), hybrid-electric regional propulsion, and active laminar flow control.
6. **Resources Needed:** NASA aeronautics technical reports and open airfoil/CFD datasets.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate lift/drag polar curves and range via the Breguet range equation.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NASA, DLR (via UNOOSA), CSIR India
