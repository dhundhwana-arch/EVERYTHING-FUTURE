# Deep Learning — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `005_DEEP_LEARNING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Theoretical explanation of grokking, benign overfitting, and energy-efficient on-device training.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Quadratic attention scaling with sequence length (in vanilla Transformers) and high GPU/memory bandwidth requirements.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** State-space models, sparse mixture-of-experts, low-bit quantization, and hardware-aware kernels.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Measure activation distributions and vanishing gradients across varying network depths on toy datasets.
