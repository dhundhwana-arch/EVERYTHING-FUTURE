## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Deep residual networks, convolutional architectures, diffusion models, and Transformers excel on unstructured perceptual and sequential data.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** 152-layer ResNet achieving 3.57% ImageNet top-5 error (He et al., 2016) and billion-parameter foundation models.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Theoretical explanation of grokking, benign overfitting, and energy-efficient on-device training.
4. **Major Limitations:** Quadratic attention scaling with sequence length (in vanilla Transformers) and high GPU/memory bandwidth requirements.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** State-space models, sparse mixture-of-experts, low-bit quantization, and hardware-aware kernels.
6. **Resources Needed:** Open frameworks (PyTorch), standardized vision/language benchmarks, and profiling tools.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Measure activation distributions and vanishing gradients across varying network depths on toy datasets.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** PyTorch Foundation, Hugging Face
