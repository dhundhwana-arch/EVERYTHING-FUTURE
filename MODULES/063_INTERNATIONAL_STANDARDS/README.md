## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** International standards (ISO, IEC, IEEE, NIST, BIS) underpin global trade, electrical safety, software, and energy management.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Global harmonization of ISO 50001 energy management, IEEE wireless/networking protocols, and open RISC-V ISA specs.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Fast-moving consensus standards for AI safety evaluation, autonomous vehicles, and post-quantum migration.
4. **Major Limitations:** Paywalled access to some standards documents and slow multi-year committee ratification cycles.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Machine-readable SMART standards, open-source reference implementations, and international mutual-recognition agreements.
6. **Resources Needed:** IEEE SA, NIST, BIS India, CERN standards library, and WTO Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT).
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Build a standards-compliance traceability matrix mapping engineering requirements to ISO/IEEE/NIST clauses.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IEEE Standards Association, NIST, BIS India, WTO, RISC-V International
