# International Standards — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `063_INTERNATIONAL_STANDARDS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Fast-moving consensus standards for AI safety evaluation, autonomous vehicles, and post-quantum migration.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Paywalled access to some standards documents and slow multi-year committee ratification cycles.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Machine-readable SMART standards, open-source reference implementations, and international mutual-recognition agreements.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Build a standards-compliance traceability matrix mapping engineering requirements to ISO/IEEE/NIST clauses.
