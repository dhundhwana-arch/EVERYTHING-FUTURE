# International Standards — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `063_INTERNATIONAL_STANDARDS` | **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Consensus Standard** | Technical specification developed via transparent multi-stakeholder review to ensure safety and interoperability. |
| **Conformity Assessment** | Testing, inspection, and certification verifying a product or process meets a referenced standard. |
| **Metrology** | Science of measurement ensuring SI unit traceability across laboratories and borders (NIST/BIPM). |
| **Interoperability** | Capability of two or more systems to exchange and meaningfully use information (ISO/IEC/IEEE 24765). |
