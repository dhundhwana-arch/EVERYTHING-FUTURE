## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Non-invasive EEG spellers and clinical invasive BCIs have restored basic cursor control and speech synthesis in trials.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** High-rate speech decoding for paralyzed patients and closed-loop deep-brain stimulation for Parkinson's disease.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Decade-long stable high-channel implants, non-invasive single-neuron resolution, and universal neuroprivacy protections.
4. **Major Limitations:** Signal degradation from glial scarring, surgical risk for invasive systems, and low SNR in scalp EEG.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Flexible polymer microelectrodes, endovascular stent-electrodes, and adaptive self-calibrating decoders.
6. **Resources Needed:** PubMed/NCBI clinical BCI trials and IEEE neuroengineering standards.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Apply bandpass filtering and FFT power-spectral density estimation (alpha/beta bands) to synthetic EEG signals.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NCBI / NIH, IEEE Standards Association
