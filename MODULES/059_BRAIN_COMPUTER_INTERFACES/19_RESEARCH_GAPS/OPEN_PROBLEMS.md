# Brain-Computer Interfaces — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `059_BRAIN_COMPUTER_INTERFACES` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Decade-long stable high-channel implants, non-invasive single-neuron resolution, and universal neuroprivacy protections.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Signal degradation from glial scarring, surgical risk for invasive systems, and low SNR in scalp EEG.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Flexible polymer microelectrodes, endovascular stent-electrodes, and adaptive self-calibrating decoders.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Apply bandpass filtering and FFT power-spectral density estimation (alpha/beta bands) to synthetic EEG signals.
