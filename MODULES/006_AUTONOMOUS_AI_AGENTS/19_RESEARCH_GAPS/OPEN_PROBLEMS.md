# Autonomous AI Agents — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `006_AUTONOMOUS_AI_AGENTS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Long-horizon reliability (preventing compounding step errors), prompt-injection defense, and multi-agent coordination.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Error accumulation over long trajectories, brittle tool-schema parsing, and security risks in privileged environments.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Formal state-machine guardrails, sandboxed tool execution, verification-guided planning, and episodic memory.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Run a deterministic finite-state ReAct agent simulation with step budgets and verify safe termination.
