## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Statistical learning, Transformer self-attention, reinforcement learning, and formal search provide strong narrow and multimodal capabilities.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Large-scale multimodal models, automated protein folding (AlphaFold 2), and high-accuracy translation/coding systems.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Reliable out-of-distribution causal reasoning, guaranteed factual truthfulness, and sample-efficient continual learning.
4. **Major Limitations:** High training/inference energy demand, brittleness under distribution shift, and opaque internal representations.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Neuro-symbolic verification, mechanistic interpretability, test-time compute scaling, and small specialized models.
6. **Resources Needed:** Curated open datasets, reproducible evaluation harnesses, and transparent compute benchmarks.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Evaluate classifier calibration, confusion matrices, and prompt robustness using local synthetic datasets.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** PyTorch Foundation, Hugging Face, Max Planck Society, NIST
