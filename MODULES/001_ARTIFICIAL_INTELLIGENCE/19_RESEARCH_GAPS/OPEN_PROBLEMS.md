# Artificial Intelligence — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `001_ARTIFICIAL_INTELLIGENCE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Reliable out-of-distribution causal reasoning, guaranteed factual truthfulness, and sample-efficient continual learning.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** High training/inference energy demand, brittleness under distribution shift, and opaque internal representations.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Neuro-symbolic verification, mechanistic interpretability, test-time compute scaling, and small specialized models.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Evaluate classifier calibration, confusion matrices, and prompt robustness using local synthetic datasets.
