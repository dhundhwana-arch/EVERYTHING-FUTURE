# Artificial Intelligence — Mathematical Foundations

## Key Mathematical Foundations
- **Scaled Dot-Product Attention:** `Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax((Q K^T) / sqrt(d_k)) V` (Vaswani et al., 2017).
- **Expected Calibration Error:** `ECE = sum_b (|B_b| / n) * |acc(B_b) - conf(B_b)|`.
- **F1 Score:** Harmonic mean of precision and recall: `F1 = 2 * P * R / (P + R)`.
