# Artificial Intelligence — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `001_ARTIFICIAL_INTELLIGENCE` | **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Transformer** | Sequence architecture relying on self-attention mechanisms rather than recurrence. |
| **Calibration (ECE)** | Expected Calibration Error measuring alignment between predicted confidence and empirical accuracy. |
| **Inference** | Executing a trained model on unseen inputs to produce predictions or actions. |
| **Alignment** | Ensuring AI system objectives and behaviors remain consistent with human intent and safety constraints. |
| **Hallucination** | Generation of plausible-sounding outputs that contradict verifiable facts or provided context. |
