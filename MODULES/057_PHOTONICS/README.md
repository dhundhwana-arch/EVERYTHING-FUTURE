## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Semiconductor lasers, fiber-optic communications, and integrated photonic circuits (PICs) are commercially mature.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** IMEC/TSMC silicon photonics wafer platforms and 800G/1.6T optical datacenter interconnects.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Monolithic on-chip laser integration on silicon, ultra-low-loss photonic switches, and optical computing scalability.
4. **Major Limitations:** Thermal sensitivity of microring resonators and fiber-to-chip coupling packaging costs.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Co-packaged optics for AI clusters, lithium-niobate thin-film modulators, and solid-state photonic LiDAR.
6. **Resources Needed:** IMEC silicon photonics programs and IEEE Photonics standards.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Calculate Snell's law critical angle and optical waveguide numerical aperture.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IMEC, TSMC, IEEE Standards Association
