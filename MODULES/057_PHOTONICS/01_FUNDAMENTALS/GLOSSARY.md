# Photonics — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `057_PHOTONICS` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Silicon Photonics** | Fabricating optical waveguides, modulators, and detectors on silicon wafers using CMOS processes. |
| **Refractive Index** | Ratio of speed of light in vacuum to phase velocity in a medium, enabling total internal reflection. |
| **Optical Transceiver** | Device converting electrical data streams into modulated laser pulses and back. |
