# Photonics — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `057_PHOTONICS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Monolithic on-chip laser integration on silicon, ultra-low-loss photonic switches, and optical computing scalability.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Thermal sensitivity of microring resonators and fiber-to-chip coupling packaging costs.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Co-packaged optics for AI clusters, lithium-niobate thin-film modulators, and solid-state photonic LiDAR.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate Snell's law critical angle and optical waveguide numerical aperture.
