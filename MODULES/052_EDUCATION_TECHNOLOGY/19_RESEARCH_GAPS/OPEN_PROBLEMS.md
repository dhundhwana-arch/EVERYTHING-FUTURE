# Education Technology — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `052_EDUCATION_TECHNOLOGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Equitable high-quality tutoring in low-resource/vernacular regions without over-reliance on passive AI answers.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Digital divide (device/bandwidth access), assessment integrity, and teacher-training gaps.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Offline-first Socratic tutors, open multilingual STEM curricula, and competency-based micro-credentials.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Implement an SM-2 spaced-repetition interval scheduler in Python and simulate 90-day retention.
