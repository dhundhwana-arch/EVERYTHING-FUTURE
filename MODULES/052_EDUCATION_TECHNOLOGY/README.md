## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Active recall, spaced repetition, and worked-example pedagogy show strong empirical learning gains across disciplines.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Global open courseware, multilingual mobile learning apps, and offline-first digital textbook platforms.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Equitable high-quality tutoring in low-resource/vernacular regions without over-reliance on passive AI answers.
4. **Major Limitations:** Digital divide (device/bandwidth access), assessment integrity, and teacher-training gaps.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Offline-first Socratic tutors, open multilingual STEM curricula, and competency-based micro-credentials.
6. **Resources Needed:** Open educational repositories and cognitive science literature on PubMed/arXiv.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Implement an SM-2 spaced-repetition interval scheduler in Python and simulate 90-day retention.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** Academic OER Communities, OpenAlex
