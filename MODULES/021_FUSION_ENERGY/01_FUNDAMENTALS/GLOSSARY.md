# Fusion Energy — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `021_FUSION_ENERGY` | **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lawson Triple Product** | Product n * T * tau_E (density * temperature * energy confinement time) required for ignition. |
| **Tokamak** | Toroidal magnetic confinement chamber using toroidal and poloidal fields to confine hot plasma. |
| **Q-Factor** | Ratio of fusion power output to external heating power input (Q > 1 scientific breakeven). |
| **Tritium Breeding Ratio (TBR)** | Ratio of tritium generated in lithium blankets to tritium consumed in D-T fusion (must exceed 1.0). |
