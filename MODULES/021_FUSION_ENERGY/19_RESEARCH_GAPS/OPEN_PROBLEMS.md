# Fusion Energy — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `021_FUSION_ENERGY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Achieving net-electric engineering gain (Q_eng > 1), resilient 14.1 MeV neutron-resistant first-wall materials, and closed tritium fuel cycles.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Plasma disruptions/ELMs, wall-plug laser efficiency (~1% in ICF), and extreme magnet/cryogenic engineering complexity.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** High-temperature superconducting (HTS) compact tokamaks, optimized stellarators, and liquid-metal divertors.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate D-T Lawson triple product, plasma Q vs engineering Q_eng, and wall-plug efficiency requirements.
