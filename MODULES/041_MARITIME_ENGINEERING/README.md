## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Hull resistance testing, containerization logistics, and marine diesel/dual-fuel propulsion dominate global freight (~80% of trade by volume).
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Dual-fuel methanol container vessels, wind-assisted rotor sails, and automated port gantry cranes.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Global bunkering infrastructure for zero-carbon marine fuels and autonomous ocean-crossing navigation.
4. **Major Limitations:** Long 25–30 year vessel lifespans and ammonia toxicity handling requirements.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Wind-assisted propulsion, shore-power cold ironing, and digital-twin weather routing.
6. **Resources Needed:** WTO maritime trade reports and IEEE marine engineering standards.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Estimate ship hull speed and wave-making resistance scaling via Froude number.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** WTO, IEEE Standards Association
