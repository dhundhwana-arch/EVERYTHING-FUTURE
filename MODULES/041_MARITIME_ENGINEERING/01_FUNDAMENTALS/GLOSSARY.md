# Maritime Engineering — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `041_MARITIME_ENGINEERING` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Deadweight Tonnage (DWT)** | Maximum mass of cargo, fuel, crew, and supplies a vessel can safely carry. |
| **Froude Number** | Dimensionless speed-length ratio governing ship wave-making resistance. |
| **Green Methanol / Ammonia** | Low-carbon synthetic marine fuels investigated for deep-sea shipping. |
