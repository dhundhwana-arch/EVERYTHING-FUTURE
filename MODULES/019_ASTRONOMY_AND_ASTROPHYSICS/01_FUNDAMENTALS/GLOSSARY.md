# Astronomy and Astrophysics — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `019_ASTRONOMY_AND_ASTROPHYSICS` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Redshift (z)** | Fractional increase in photon wavelength caused by cosmic expansion or relative motion. |
| **Exoplanet Transit** | Periodic dip in stellar brightness as an orbiting planet crosses its host star's disk. |
| **Multi-Messenger Astronomy** | Joint observation of cosmic events via photons, gravitational waves, and neutrinos. |
