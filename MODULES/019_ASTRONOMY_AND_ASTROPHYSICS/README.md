## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Lambda-CDM cosmology, stellar nucleosynthesis, and general relativity match precision observations across vast scales.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Direct imaging/spectroscopy of exoplanet atmospheres, gravitational-wave detections, and deep-field infrared telescopes.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Nature of dark matter and dark energy, Hubble tension, and mechanisms of fast radio bursts.
4. **Major Limitations:** Atmospheric distortion for ground telescopes, faint signal-to-noise ratios, and petabyte-scale data reduction.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Next-generation 30m-class ground telescopes, space interferometry, and AI-assisted transient detection.
6. **Resources Needed:** NASA/ESA/CERN open astrophysics archives and arXiv astro-ph preprints.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Analyze synthetic exoplanet transit light curves to estimate planet-to-star radius ratio.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NASA, CERN, Max Planck Society
