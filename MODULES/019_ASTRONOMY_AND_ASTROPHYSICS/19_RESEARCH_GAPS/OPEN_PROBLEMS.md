# Astronomy and Astrophysics — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `019_ASTRONOMY_AND_ASTROPHYSICS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Nature of dark matter and dark energy, Hubble tension, and mechanisms of fast radio bursts.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Atmospheric distortion for ground telescopes, faint signal-to-noise ratios, and petabyte-scale data reduction.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Next-generation 30m-class ground telescopes, space interferometry, and AI-assisted transient detection.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Analyze synthetic exoplanet transit light curves to estimate planet-to-star radius ratio.
