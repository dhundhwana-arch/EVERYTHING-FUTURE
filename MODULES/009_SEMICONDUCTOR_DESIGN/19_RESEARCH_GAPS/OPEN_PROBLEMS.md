# Semiconductor Design — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `009_SEMICONDUCTOR_DESIGN` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Overcoming the end of Dennard scaling, interconnect RC bottleneck, and rising verification complexity.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Multi-million-dollar mask/NRE costs at leading nodes and dark-silicon thermal density limits.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Open-source RISC-V silicon, AI-assisted place-and-route, backside power delivery, and 2D channel transistors.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Calculate CMOS dynamic power, RC delay, and frequency scaling tradeoffs across supply voltages.
