# Rocket Science — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `017_ROCKET_SCIENCE` | **Verification Status:** `VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tsiolkovsky Equation** | Delta-v = Isp * g0 * ln(m0 / mf) relating velocity gain to specific impulse and mass ratio. |
| **Mass Ratio** | Ratio of initial wet mass (m0) to final dry mass (mf) of a rocket stage. |
| **De Laval Nozzle** | Converging-diverging nozzle accelerating hot exhaust gases to supersonic velocities. |
| **Multi-Stage Rocket** | Discarding empty propellant tanks mid-flight to dramatically improve payload mass fraction. |
