# Rocket Science — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `017_ROCKET_SCIENCE` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Single-stage-to-orbit viability, rapid aircraft-like turnaround, and high-thrust nuclear thermal/electric propulsion.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Exponential mass penalty of the rocket equation and severe acoustic/thermal structural loads.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Full-flow staged combustion reuse, additive manufacturing of regeneratively cooled chambers, and high-power electric propulsion.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Simulate single-stage vs two-stage rocket delta-v and payload fraction using Tsiolkovsky's equation.
