## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Liquid bipropellant engines, multi-stage ascent optimization, and Hall-effect electric thrusters are well understood.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Orbital-class booster vertical landing/reuse, cryogenic methalox engines, and high-reliability polar/geosynchronous launchers.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Single-stage-to-orbit viability, rapid aircraft-like turnaround, and high-thrust nuclear thermal/electric propulsion.
4. **Major Limitations:** Exponential mass penalty of the rocket equation and severe acoustic/thermal structural loads.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Full-flow staged combustion reuse, additive manufacturing of regeneratively cooled chambers, and high-power electric propulsion.
6. **Resources Needed:** Propulsion physics simulators and NASA/ISRO/CNSA mission documentation.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Simulate single-stage vs two-stage rocket delta-v and payload fraction using Tsiolkovsky's equation.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NASA, ISRO, CNSA
