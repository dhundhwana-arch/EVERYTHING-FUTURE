# EUV Lithography — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `011_EUV_LITHOGRAPHY` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Mitigating stochastic micro-bridging/line-breaks while maintaining high wafer throughput (>200 wafers/hour).
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Extremely high tool capital cost, multi-megawatt facility power draw, and shrinking Depth of Focus at High-NA.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Metal-oxide photoresists, anamorphic High-NA optics, and GPU-accelerated inverse lithography (OPC).
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Compute Rayleigh CD and Depth of Focus tradeoffs across 193i DUV, 0.33 NA EUV, and 0.55 NA High-NA EUV.
