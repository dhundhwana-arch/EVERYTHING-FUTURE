# Startup Ecosystems — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `066_STARTUP_ECOSYSTEMS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Financing capital-intensive hardware/climate/biotech startups with 7–10 year commercialization horizons.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** High early failure rates, intellectual-property licensing delays, and geographic concentration of venture capital.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Non-dilutive government R&D grants, shared pilot-scale fab/biofoundry access, and blended climate finance.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Model startup runway, monthly burn rate, gross margin, and break-even unit volume in Python.
