## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Lean customer discovery, milestone-gated deep-tech de-risking, and university tech-transfer offices drive venture creation.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Global startup hubs linking public research labs (IMEC, IITs/CSIR, MIT/Stanford) with seed and industrial capital.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Financing capital-intensive hardware/climate/biotech startups with 7–10 year commercialization horizons.
4. **Major Limitations:** High early failure rates, intellectual-property licensing delays, and geographic concentration of venture capital.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Non-dilutive government R&D grants, shared pilot-scale fab/biofoundry access, and blended climate finance.
6. **Resources Needed:** WIPO innovation indicators and university tech-transfer guides.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Model startup runway, monthly burn rate, gross margin, and break-even unit volume in Python.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** WIPO, IMEC, CSIR India
