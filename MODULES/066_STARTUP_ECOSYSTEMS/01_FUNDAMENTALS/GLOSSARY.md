# Startup Ecosystems — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `066_STARTUP_ECOSYSTEMS` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **TRL (Technology Readiness Level)** | 1-to-9 scale measuring maturity from basic principle (TRL 1) to proven operational system (TRL 9). |
| **Unit Economics** | Direct revenues and variable costs associated with a single customer or unit sold (LTV/CAC, gross margin). |
| **Valley of Death** | Funding gap between grant-backed academic research (TRL 3–4) and commercial venture scaling (TRL 7+). |
