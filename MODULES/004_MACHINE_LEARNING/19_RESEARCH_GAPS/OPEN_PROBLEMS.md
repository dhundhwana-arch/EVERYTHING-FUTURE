# Machine Learning — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `004_MACHINE_LEARNING` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Robust learning under non-stationary distributions, causal discovery from observational data, and private federated optimization.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Data quality bottlenecks, label noise, spurious correlations, and feature drift in production.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Causal machine learning, conformal prediction for uncertainty quantification, and differentially private training.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Run k-fold cross-validated gradient descent regression with synthetic noisy datasets and inspect learning curves.
