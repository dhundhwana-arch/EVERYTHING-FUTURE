# Quantum Communication — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `014_QUANTUM_COMMUNICATION` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Long-distance (>1000 km) high-rate quantum repeaters with long-lived room-temperature quantum memories.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Exponential photon attenuation in optical fiber and side-channel vulnerabilities in practical detectors.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Measurement-device-independent QKD, satellite quantum networks, and hybrid QKD + NIST PQC deployments.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Simulate the BB84 basis-reconciliation step and eavesdropper error-rate detection in Python.
