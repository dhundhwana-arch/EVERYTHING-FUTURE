## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Point-to-point fiber QKD and satellite-assisted entanglement links have been experimentally demonstrated.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Metropolitan QKD testbeds and free-space satellite quantum communication demonstrations.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Long-distance (>1000 km) high-rate quantum repeaters with long-lived room-temperature quantum memories.
4. **Major Limitations:** Exponential photon attenuation in optical fiber and side-channel vulnerabilities in practical detectors.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Measurement-device-independent QKD, satellite quantum networks, and hybrid QKD + NIST PQC deployments.
6. **Resources Needed:** Optical link-budget models and NIST cryptographic standards.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Simulate the BB84 basis-reconciliation step and eavesdropper error-rate detection in Python.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** NIST, IEEE Standards Association, CERN
