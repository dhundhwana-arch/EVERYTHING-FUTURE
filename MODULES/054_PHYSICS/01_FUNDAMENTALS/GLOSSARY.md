# Physics — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `054_PHYSICS` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Conservation Laws** | Invariance of energy, momentum, and charge linked to continuous symmetries via Noether's theorem. |
| **Standard Model** | Quantum field theory describing electromagnetic, weak, and strong fundamental interactions. |
| **Superconductivity** | Zero electrical resistance and magnetic flux expulsion (Meissner effect) below critical temperature Tc. |
