## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** Classical physics, quantum electrodynamics, and general relativity are tested to extraordinary precision.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Discovery of the Higgs boson at CERN LHC, gravitational-wave astronomy, and precision atomic clocks at NIST.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Unifying quantum mechanics with general relativity (quantum gravity), dark matter identity, and high-Tc mechanisms.
4. **Major Limitations:** Energy scale limits of terrestrial particle colliders and intractable many-body quantum systems.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Quantum simulation of strongly correlated matter, tabletop precision interferometry, and next-generation colliders.
6. **Resources Needed:** CERN open data, NIST physical constants (CODATA), and arXiv physics preprints.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Simulate coupled oscillators or projectile motion with quadratic air drag numerically.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** CERN, NIST, Max Planck Society
