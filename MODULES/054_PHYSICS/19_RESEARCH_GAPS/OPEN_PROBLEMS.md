# Physics — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `054_PHYSICS` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Unifying quantum mechanics with general relativity (quantum gravity), dark matter identity, and high-Tc mechanisms.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Energy scale limits of terrestrial particle colliders and intractable many-body quantum systems.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Quantum simulation of strongly correlated matter, tabletop precision interferometry, and next-generation colliders.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Simulate coupled oscillators or projectile motion with quadratic air drag numerically.
