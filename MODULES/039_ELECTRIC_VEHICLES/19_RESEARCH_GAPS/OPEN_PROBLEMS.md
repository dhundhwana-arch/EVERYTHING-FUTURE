# Electric Vehicles — Open Problems & Research Gaps

**Module:** `039_ELECTRIC_VEHICLES` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

## Unsolved Scientific & Engineering Challenges
- **Core Unsolved Bottleneck:** Commercial solid-state batteries, cold-weather range retention, and closed-loop battery recycling at scale.
- **Current Practical Limitations:** Charging infrastructure gaps in dense/rural regions and upstream lithium/cobalt/graphite refining concentration.

## Promising Safe Research Directions
- **Directions:** Sodium-ion city EVs, cell-to-pack structural batteries, and vehicle-to-grid (V2G) bidirectional storage.
- **Safe Reproducible Starter Experiment:** Estimate EV range and energy consumption (Wh/km) from aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance, and mass.
