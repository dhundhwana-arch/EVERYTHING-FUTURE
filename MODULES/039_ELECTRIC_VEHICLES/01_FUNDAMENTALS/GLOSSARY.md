# Electric Vehicles — Standalone Technical Glossary

**Module:** `039_ELECTRIC_VEHICLES` | **Verification Status:** `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` | **Last Verified:** 2026-11-19

| Term | Clear Technical Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **BMS (Battery Management System)** | Embedded controller monitoring cell voltages, temperatures, State of Charge, and safety limits. |
| **C-Rate** | Charge or discharge current normalized to battery nominal capacity (1C empties a battery in 1 hour). |
| **SiC Inverter** | Silicon-carbide power electronics switching DC battery power to AC motor drive at high efficiency. |
