## Scientific Research Intelligence (8-Point Synthesis)
1. **What Is Currently Known:** 800V SiC powertrains, LFP/NMC lithium-ion packs, and regenerative braking achieve >85% tank-to-wheel efficiency.
2. **What Has Already Been Achieved:** Global passenger EV scaling, megawatt commercial charging pilots, and battery pack cost reductions.
3. **What Remains Unsolved:** Commercial solid-state batteries, cold-weather range retention, and closed-loop battery recycling at scale.
4. **Major Limitations:** Charging infrastructure gaps in dense/rural regions and upstream lithium/cobalt/graphite refining concentration.
5. **Promising Research Directions:** Sodium-ion city EVs, cell-to-pack structural batteries, and vehicle-to-grid (V2G) bidirectional storage.
6. **Resources Needed:** IRENA transport electrification reports and IEEE charging standards.
7. **Safe Conductable Experiments:** Estimate EV range and energy consumption (Wh/km) from aerodynamic drag, rolling resistance, and mass.
8. **Active Institutions & Ecosystems:** IRENA, IEEE Standards Association
